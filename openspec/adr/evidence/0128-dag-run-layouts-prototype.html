Vertical or horizontal

One workflow, two ways to watch it. Open the ⋯ menu at the top right of the panel to switch between a vertical side panel and a horizontal bottom drawer, or to remove the workflow. Each layout collapses on its own. Hover or tap a step to trace what it waits on and what it unblocks.

Show as Vertical or Horizontal, then Remove workflow. It's on the expanded header and on both collapsed forms, so you never have to expand to switch.

Vertical · side panel

Quiet stages. Collapsed it's a 36px rail, 8px wider for each bracket nested inside another. Expanded it uses the panel's chosen width.

Horizontal · bottom drawer

Metro map. Collapsed it's a 46px strip: the shape in dots, plus what's running now. Expanded it scrolls sideways inside the drawer only when the plan is wider than the pane.

One choice for every terminal

The layout is a single preference, so every workflow looks the same everywhere. Collapsed or expanded is remembered for each layout separately.
